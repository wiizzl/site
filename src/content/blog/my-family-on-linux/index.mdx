My mother, my father and my brother all used Windows. Today, they all use Linux.

## Why

For a while now, I've been trying to depend as little as possible on big tech companies. Moving my own machines was the easy part. Moving my family's was the real test: if it works for people who don't care about operating systems, it works.

## What they use

They run [Aurora](https://getaurora.dev), through the `family` image of my [Atomic](/projects/atomic/) setup. A new version of the system is built every week, and if an update ever breaks something, the previous version is one reboot away.
