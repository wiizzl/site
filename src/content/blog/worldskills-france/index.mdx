## What is WorldSkills?

WorldSkills is a competition of trades for young people under 23. In France, it starts with regional selections, then the best competitors of each region meet at the national finals. Medallists can then try to join the French team for the European (EuroSkills) and world (WorldSkills) competitions.

The 48th national finals took place from October 16 to 18, 2025, at the Parc Chanot in Marseille: more than 800 young people from 14 regions, in around 70 trades. Normandy sent 59 competitors and came back with 34 medals.

## Web Technologies, skill 17

Web Technologies (skill 17) is the web development trade. Over several days, you go through timed modules: building a frontend from a design, a backend and an API, sometimes a fullstack app, plus speed modules.

## Want to get involved?

Registrations go through the [WorldSkills France website](https://inscriptions.worldskills-france.org), whether you want to compete or to volunteer. To compete, you need to be under 23, and to sign up before your region's selections.

If you're hesitating, or if you want to know what Web Technologies really looks like from the inside, [send me a message](/). I'll be happy to tell you more.
